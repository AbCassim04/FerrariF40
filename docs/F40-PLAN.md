# F40 build plan

Plan and build checklist, written 2026-11-12, approved 2026-11-13 (answers to the open
questions in docs/BRIEF.md's decision log); phases 2–4 built on `wip/f40-build`. Tick each item when it exists as a
registry part (or instanced sub-part) with an info-panel entry. Layout figures:
docs/f40-layout-1.svg (engine bay) and docs/f40-layout-2.svg (whole car), generated by
`node --import ./scripts/ts-hooks.mjs scripts/layout-sketch.mjs`; envelope key in
docs/f40-layout-key.json. Every planned envelope is clash-tested against the fixed bodywork
and the wheels; the sketch's output lists any hit (currently none outside the declared
contacts).

**Reuse.** Several builders from the LaFerrari project are a starting point. They were
removed from this tree in phase 1 (they were LaFerrari layouts) and live in this repo's
history at commit `b851fa8` (bring one back with `git show b851fa8:<path> > <path>`):
`src/parts/engine/geometry/intakeExhaust.ts` (runners, throttle bodies, fuel rails, ribbed
bellows, header primaries with flanges, silencer, tips, ECU, fuse box, loom),
`src/parts/engine/exhaustLayout.ts` (primary routing), `src/parts/drivetrain/geometry.ts`
(flywheel, clutch, bellhousing, lofted ribbed casing, gear clusters, diff, driveshafts) and
`src/parts/cooling.ts` (re-curving hoses on the cable system). "adapt" below names the one
to start from; "new" has no predecessor.

## Decisions for review
1. **Engine placement (provisional, built).** Crank centreline 0.27 m above the ground;
   belt-cover face at z −0.40 (30 mm clear of the bay's front wall at −0.37…−0.41); block
   rear face at z −0.858; sump bottom 0.154. Across the cam covers 0.62 m in a bay ±0.40
   wide; cover tops at y 0.58, under the shelf (0.79) and the Lexan (≈1.0). No contact with
   the body (clash check C). The front ~0.25 m of the engine sits under the roof's trailing
   edge, as on the real car, seen through the Lexan from behind.
2. **Turbos inboard of the rear coilovers.** The model's stand-in turbos and coilovers
   overlap. Recommend turbos at x ±0.24…0.46 (behind the bank, over the driveshafts), the
   coilovers at x ±0.47…0.60, as in figure 1/2. Exhaust manifolds run back outboard and
   below each bank to them.
3. **Wastegates: two.** mitorosso lists "1 wastegate valve"; parts sources point to one per
   turbo, joined into the centre tailpipe. Recommend two (one per turbo), merged 2-into-1.
4. **Rear frame rails.** The stand-in's upper rails (x ±0.35…0.39, y ≈0.42) run exactly where
   the manifolds must go. Recommend the procedural frame's upper rails outboard of them
   (x ±0.41…0.46); lower rails as the stand-in (x ±0.30…0.35, y ≈0.15).
5. **Rear track.** Geometry follows the model's wheels (rear track 1.563 m, 47 mm narrower
   than the 1.610 reference; the model keeps its own proportions at the wheelbase scale).
   Info panels quote the reference.
6. **Tyres.** The model uses one tyre size all round (Ø 650 mm); real: 628 front, 666 rear.
   Keep the model's wheels; info panels quote 245/40 ZR17 and 335/35 ZR17.
7. **The stand-in chassis goes piece by piece.** `model-chassis` (frame rails, suspension,
   radiator) stays visible until our part replaces each region; the prep rules then send that
   region to the hidden ghost.
8. **Ignition.** Distributors (one per bank, driven off a cam at the flywheel end, est) with
   HT leads to the plug caps already built. Coil-on-plug stays available as a parameter.
9. **Explode.** The rear clam is the reveal (built in phase 1, see "Explode choreography").
   Stage 2 slides the powertrain 0.3 m back from under the roof edge before stage 3 lifts it.
   The turbo and exhaust teardown fits inside stage 4's 1.35 s of delays and the transmission
   fills stage 5 (built: stages re-timed to 0 / 0.75 / 1.70 / 2.50 / 3.75 s; the explode
   ends at ≈ 4.9 s).
10. **Clash check.** Each new part lists its contacts (through the bulkhead, caliper on its
    disc, tailpipes through the grille, suspension through the wheelhouse liners). Full
    explode and mid-explode sweeps (`clashAt`) must stay clean, as for phase 1.

## Layout
Figure 1 (engine bay, z −2.25…−0.25) and figure 2 (whole car) show the measured bays
(dashed), the model's stand-in powertrain (cyan cells) and stand-in frame/suspension (violet
cells), the engine as built (solid) and every planned envelope (dashed, numbered; key in
docs/f40-layout-key.json):
- **Front of the bay (z −0.40…−0.86):** the V8 as built, the plenum and runners in the V,
  distributors and throttle bodies at its rear end, the alternator low on the left at the
  timing end, ECUs high on the bay walls.
- **Behind the block (z −0.86…−1.0):** flywheel, twin-plate clutch and bellhousing on the
  crank axis (0.27).
- **Over the rear axle (z −1.1…−1.65):** the LSD on the axle line inside the casing,
  driveshafts nearly level to the hubs (wheel centre 0.325), turbos either side above them,
  inboard of the coilovers; intercoolers over the turbos under the rear deck; charge pipes
  forward over the gearbox to the throttle bodies.
- **Tail (z −1.65…−2.1):** gearbox casing ends at −1.8, over the rising diffuser; silencer
  box across the tail, three tailpipes through the rear grille.
- **Frame:** lower rails x ±0.30…0.35 along the floor, upper rails outboard of the
  manifolds, crossmember over the axle (y 0.74), roll structure behind the seats, front
  frame under the footwells and front compartment.

## Explode choreography
The reveal is the rear clam: it opens about its tail hinge exactly as the real one does and
the engine is there under it, then the whole tail lifts away.

| stage | starts | what moves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 body (built) | 0.00 | **Rear clam opens 70° about its tail hinge** (the Lexan cover and the wing with it); 0.45 s: lifts up and back. 0.15 s: doors swing open about their front hinges. 0.25 s: front clam tips up about its nose and moves forward. 0.35 s: windscreen and fixed glass lift. |
| 2 chassis (partly built) | 0.75 | Wheels out 0.75, discs and calipers follow, suspension out, driveshafts out; 0.25 s: **powertrain slides back 0.3 m** from under the roof edge (once the driveshafts are clear of the exhaust collectors). |
| 3 powertrain (built) | 1.70 | Engine, gearbox, turbos, intercoolers and exhaust lift 1.2 m as one unit; the frame stays. |
| 4 engine teardown | 2.50 | Outside in, see below. |
| 5 transmission (built) | 3.75 | Starts before the crank drops (the crank's rear flange sits in the bellhousing's mouth). Gearbox slides back 0.5 off the engine (its oil cooler comes off on the way); then the flywheel and the clutch's discs and plates fan out behind the block; LSD shown in the casing. |

### Stage 4 teardown (s after the stage starts)
- +0.00: Intercoolers lift
- +0.10: Plenum lifts; charge pipes lift; turbo inlets out
- +0.20: Throttle bodies, fuel rails, distributors and HT leads; turbos out with their heat shields; downpipes out, wastegate pipe up, silencer down
- +0.25: Wastegates lift off the collectors
- +0.30: Belt cover, water pumps and alternator forward
- +0.35: Exhaust manifolds pull off their studs
- +0.45: Cam covers with plug caps
- +0.50: Runners
- +0.55: Oil tank out
- +0.60: Camshafts; spark plugs
- +0.75: Heads (valves ride); head gaskets float between
- +1.05: Sump drops (pump and pick-ups ride); filter, coolers, mounts out
- +1.20: Pistons and rods rise along each bank axis
- +1.35: Crankshaft drops
